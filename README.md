# Where Did I Put It?

A lightweight, ad-free, offline-ready home & family organizer web app to track where important items (keys, documents, tools, medicines, and household essentials) are stored.

## Key Features
- **Home Dashboard**: Quick stats (`Saved`, `Pinned`, `Lent Out`, `Rooms`), alerts for items **Out of Spot** or **Expiring Soon**, and **Pinned Essentials**.
- **Visual Spot Pin**: Attach a photo of a cupboard or shelf and tap directly on the image to place a pulsing `📍` marker on the exact spot.
- **Live Family Cloud Sync**: Create a unique **Family Group Code** or join an existing family group to sync shared items across phones in real time.
- **Private PIN Lock**: Protect sensitive items behind a 4-digit PIN (private items stay strictly on your own device and never sync to the cloud).
- **Taken Out / Put Back & Lent Tracking**: Track items temporarily taken out of their spot or lent to someone with borrower name and date.
- **Expiry Alerts & Calendar Reminders**: Add expiry/check-up dates, enable app alerts, and save 1-tap reminders to Google Calendar or `.ics`.
- **Box & Room QR Labels**: Generate printable QR codes for storage boxes or rooms—scanning opens the app filtered to that room.
- **Voice Search, WhatsApp Share & Backups**: Search or add by voice, share item photos & locations to WhatsApp, restore from **Recently Deleted**, and **Export / Merge** JSON backups.
- **100% Offline Ready & Dark Mode**: Installs as a home-screen app and works offline.
